I am a PhD student from Kazakhstan at the National Institute of Genetics, Japan. In 2022, I joined Biological Networks laboratory led by Masanori Arita.

I am studying the metagenome of various animals with bioinformatics methods, focusing on the naked mole-rat (*Heterocephalus glaber*).

In 2024, I became a visiting PhD student at Kyoko Miura's lab. We started our collaboration since 2022, but I moved to their lab to learn more about wet-lab methods.
